Privacy & legal

What SKTV is

This app does not provide channels, playlists, or stream URLs. You bring your own source. We do not support illegal services. We support the player, not the reseller.

SKTV is a media player. It plays streams from sources that you configure. It does not include, index, recommend, resell or link to any channel, playlist or stream, and it is not affiliated with any IPTV provider, broadcaster or reseller. You are responsible for making sure the sources you add are ones you're licensed to use.

What SKTV collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no telemetry. The app talks to three kinds of servers: the sources you add (to fetch your playlist, guide and video), GitHub (to check for updates and download them), and nothing else.

What stays on your machine

Your playlists, credentials, favourites, guide data, recordings and settings live in a local database in your user profile. Credentials are stored encrypted. Logs and diagnostic reports redact passwords, tokens and MAC addresses before anything is written or shown.

This website

Static pages. The download buttons ask GitHub's public API for the newest release; no cookies, no trackers.

Source code

SKTV is developed in the open at github.com/samsondebug/desktop-iptv.
