What it is

Native playback

libmpv renders straight to the GPU — no browser video tag in the path. Hardware decoding (D3D11 / VideoToolbox), ~1 s channel changes, Low Latency and Stable cache profiles.

Live + guide on one canvas

Watch a channel while you scroll the full 7-day EPG. Now/Next card, favorites, recents, global search across 40,000+ channels.

Your source, three ways

Xtream Codes, M3U/M3U8, and Stalker/MAC portals (experimental). Multiple playlists, multiple EPGs, EPG offset and overrides.

Record, download, resume

Record live TV (scheduled from the guide, with overrun), download movies and episodes with resume, continue watching, auto-next.

Mini player & multiscreen

Picture-in-picture that remembers its spot; a second pane for a second channel; parental PIN and keyword filter.

Diagnostics that settle it

HTTP trace, stream probe and source checks tell you whether buffering is the app, the network or the provider. Exports are redacted.

Install

Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)
  1. Download the -setup.exe and run it. It installs per-user (no admin) or for all users. Had the app while it was still called desktop-iptv? The installer replaces it and keeps your playlists and settings.
  2. The installer is code-signed (publisher David Krouskoff, verified by Microsoft). While the certificate is new, SmartScreen may still show Windows protected your PC once: click More info → Run anyway. Edge may ask you to Keep the download.
  3. WebView2 is fetched automatically if your PC does not have it (most do).
macOS 11+
  1. Open the .dmg for your Mac (Apple Silicon = aarch64, Intel = x64) and drag the app to Applications.
  2. The app is not notarized yet: on first launch, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
Linux
  1. .deb (Debian/Ubuntu) pulls in libmpv2; for the .AppImage install libmpv2 (or libmpv1) with your package manager first.

Installed copies check for updates on start and install them in place. Nothing is sent anywhere else: no analytics, no accounts.

First run

  1. Read and accept the player-only notice.
  2. Add your source: Xtream (server URL, username, password), an M3U URL or file, or a Stalker portal + MAC (Settings → Playlists → Experimental).
  3. Pick a channel. Press F for fullscreen, I for the stats HUD, R to record, / to search.
  4. Buffering? Open Diagnostics (Shift+D) and change one variable at a time.
