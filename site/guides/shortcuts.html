This app does not provide channels, playlists, or stream URLs. You bring your own source. We do not support illegal services. We support the player, not the reseller.
SKTV (formerly desktop-iptv) is an independent project and is not affiliated with any IPTV provider, broadcaster or reseller. No analytics, no accounts, no data collection.
